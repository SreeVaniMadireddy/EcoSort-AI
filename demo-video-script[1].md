# Two-minute demo video script

Record the real application. Do not claim unimplemented features.

**0:00–0:15 — Introduction**  
"Hello. We are presenting EcoSort AI, our Smart Recycling and Waste Segregation Assistant for the Environment and Climate problem statement."

**0:15–0:30 — Problem**  
"People may be unsure how to separate common waste items. Incorrect segregation can contaminate recyclable materials. Our prototype provides simple category-based guidance."

**0:30–1:15 — Demonstration**  
"Here I select Plastic and click Get segregation advice. The application displays the suggested collection stream, handling guidance and next step. Now I select Organic waste and show the updated guidance. I will also demonstrate the voice output."

**1:15–1:40 — Current status**  
"This version implements manual category selection, rule-based guidance and browser text-to-speech. It does not yet perform automatic image recognition."

**1:40–2:00 — Future work**  
"Our planned development includes a trained image classifier, a Llama-family advisory component using ExecuTorch on Arm, and investigation of FPGA acceleration using Verilog after selecting compatible hardware. Thank you."

## Recording checklist
- Prefer 1920x1080, 16:9 and MP4.
- Keep the video close to two minutes.
- Ensure screen text and audio are clear.
- Upload to YouTube or Google Drive and set access to Public.
- Test the link in an incognito/private browser window.
